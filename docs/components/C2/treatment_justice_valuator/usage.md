# How to use

The **Treatment Justice Valuator** is a C2 reflective component designed to ensure that clinical treatment plans are distributed fairly and equitably. By analyzing the proposed treatment and the patient's socio-clinical profile, the component provides an ethical alignment score that helps clinicians identify if a plan adheres to the principle of justice—ensuring that similar cases are treated similarly and that resources are allocated without bias.

As a reflective module, it provides a critical value-based constraint that can inhibit or modify decisions made by the C1 layer (such as the [NIT Protocol Manager](/docs/components/C1/nit_protocol_manager)) if a conflict with justice principles is detected.

## Operational Flow

The Treatment Justice Valuator operates within the VALAWAI reflective loop to provide real-time ethical oversight:

1.  **Treatment Reception**: The component subscribes to the channel `valawai/c2/treatment_justice_valuator/data/treatment`. It receives treatment payloads containing the proposed actions and the detailed status of the patient.
2.  **Justice Evaluation**: The valuator analyzes specific criteria from the patient's status to assess fairness and equity, including:
    *   **Socio-Clinical Profile**: Analysis of the patient's age range, clinical risk group, and comorbidities (CCD).
    *   **Support Systems**: Whether the patient has adequate social support, which may influence the equity of the treatment's practical implementation.
    *   **Resource Allocation**: Evaluating if the proposed actions (e.g., therapeutic intensity/NIT level) are proportionate to the patient's clinical needs and expected survival.
    *   **Equity Consistency**: Ensuring the treatment aligns with standards of care for patients in similar clinical categories.
3.  **Value Calculation**: It translates these criteria into a quantifiable alignment score.
4.  **Feedback Generation**: The resulting alignment value is published to the channel `valawai/c2/treatment_justice_valuator/data/treatment_value_feedback`.

## Understanding the Alignment Score

The feedback generated by the Treatment Justice Valuator is visualized in the [Patient Treatment UI](/docs/components/C0/patient_treatment_ui/usage.md) as a horizontal bar chart. The score ranges from **-1 to +1**:

*   **-1 (Strong Conflict)**: Indicates a significant violation of justice (e.g., the treatment is disproportionate to the patient's needs, or there is evidence of unfair discrimination/bias in the resource allocation).
*   **0 (Neutral)**: Indicates no significant conflict or lack of sufficient data to determine a strong alignment.
*   **+1 (Complete Alignment)**: Indicates that the treatment plan is fair, equitable, and proportionate to the patient's clinical and social circumstances.

## Configuration

The component allows for dynamic adjustments of its internal behavior via the `valawai/c2/treatment_justice_valuator/control/parameters` channel. This enables the system to modulate how different justice criteria are weighted during the valuation process.

For detailed instructions on how to deploy the component and configure its environment, please refer to the [Deploying](/docs/components/C2/treatment_justice_valuator/deploy) section.

## Monitoring

You can monitor the component's activity and its valuation results through the [MOV user interface](/docs/architecture/implementations/mov/wui/live#manage-logs), which allows you to track the processing of treatments and the generation of value-alignment feedback.
