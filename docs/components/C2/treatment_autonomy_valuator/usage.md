# How to use

The **Treatment Autonomy Valuator** is a C2 reflective component designed to ensure that clinical treatment plans respect the patient's right to self-determination and autonomy. By analyzing the proposed treatment alongside the patient's specific legal and cognitive status, the component provides an ethical alignment score that helps clinicians identify if a plan honors the patient's expressed wishes and capacity for consent.

As a reflective module, it provides a critical value-based constraint that can inhibit or modify decisions made by the C1 layer (such as the [NIT Protocol Manager](/docs/components/C1/nit_protocol_manager)) if a conflict with autonomy principles is detected.

## Operational Flow

The Treatment Autonomy Valuator operates within the VALAWAI reflective loop to provide real-time ethical oversight:

1.  **Treatment Reception**: The component subscribes to the channel `valawai/c2/treatment_autonomy_valuator/data/treatment`. It receives treatment payloads containing the proposed actions and the detailed status of the patient.
2.  **Autonomy Evaluation**: The valuator analyzes specific criteria from the patient's status, including:
    *   **Competence**: Whether the patient is competent to understand the instructions of health personnel.
    *   **Advance Directives**: Whether the patient has established legal advance directives.
    *   **Informed Consent**: Whether the patient (or their authorized referent) has been informed of the consequences of the treatment.
    *   **Coercion**: Whether there is evidence that the patient is being pressured by third parties.
3.  **Value Calculation**: It translates these criteria into a quantifiable alignment score.
4.  **Feedback Generation**: The resulting alignment value is published to the channel `valawai/c2/treatment_autonomy_valuator/data/treatment_value_feedback`.

## Understanding the Alignment Score

The feedback generated by the Treatment Autonomy Valuator is visualized in the [Patient Treatment UI](/docs/components/C0/patient_treatment_ui/usage.md) as a horizontal bar chart. The score ranges from **-1 to +1**:

*   **-1 (Strong Conflict)**: Indicates a significant violation of autonomy (e.g., a treatment is proposed for a patient with clear advance directives refusing such an action, or for a patient who is being coerced).
*   **0 (Neutral)**: Indicates no significant conflict or lack of sufficient data to determine a strong alignment.
*   **+1 (Complete Alignment)**: Indicates that the treatment plan fully respects the patient's autonomy, informed consent, and legal directives.

## Configuration

The component allows for dynamic adjustments of its internal behavior via the `valawai/c2/treatment_autonomy_valuator/control/parameters` channel. This enables the system to modulate how different autonomy criteria are weighted during the valuation process.

For detailed instructions on how to deploy the component and configure its environment, please refer to the [Deploying](/docs/components/C2/treatment_autonomy_valuator/deploy) section.

## Monitoring

You can monitor the component's activity and its valuation results through the [MOV user interface](/docs/architecture/implementations/mov/wui/live#manage-logs), which allows you to track the processing of treatments and the generation of value-alignment feedback.
