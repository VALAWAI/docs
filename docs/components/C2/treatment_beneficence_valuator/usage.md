# How to use

The **Treatment Beneficence Valuator** is a C2 reflective component designed to ensure that clinical treatment plans act in the patient's best interest, maximizing benefit and promoting well-being. By analyzing the proposed treatment, the patient's current status, and the expected outcome, the component provides an ethical alignment score that helps clinicians identify if a plan adheres to the principle of beneficence.

As a reflective module, it provides a critical value-based constraint that can inhibit or modify decisions made by the C1 layer (such as the [NIT Protocol Manager](/docs/components/C1/nit_protocol_manager)) if a conflict with beneficence principles is detected.

## Operational Flow

The Treatment Beneficence Valuator operates within the VALAWAI reflective loop to provide real-time ethical oversight:

1.  **Treatment Reception**: The component subscribes to the channel `valawai/c2/treatment_beneficence_valuator/data/treatment`. It receives treatment payloads containing the proposed actions, the `before_status` (current state), and the `expected_status` of the patient.
2.  **Beneficence Evaluation**: The valuator analyzes the transition from the current status to the expected status, considering criteria such as:
    *   **Expected Survival**: Impact on the patient's life expectancy.
    *   **Clinical Risk Group**: Alignment with the appropriate risk management for the patient's category.
    *   **Functional Independence**: Improvements or maintenance of independence (instrumental and admission status).
    *   **Symptom Relief**: Reduction of emotional pain or discomfort degrees.
    *   **Overall Status Improvement**: Whether the `expected_status` represents a net gain in health or quality of life compared to the `before_status`.
3.  **Value Calculation**: It translates these improvements and outcomes into a quantifiable alignment score.
4.  **Feedback Generation**: The resulting alignment value is published to the channel `valawai/c2/treatment_beneficence_valuator/data/treatment_value_feedback`.

## Understanding the Alignment Score

The feedback generated by the Treatment Beneficence Valuator is visualized in the [Patient Treatment UI](/docs/components/C0/patient_treatment_ui/usage.md) as a horizontal bar chart. The score ranges from **-1 to +1**:

*   **-1 (Strong Conflict)**: Indicates that the treatment is likely to result in a worse outcome or provides no benefit while imposing burden (e.g., aggressive treatment in a patient with very low expected survival and high fragility).
*   **0 (Neutral)**: Indicates no significant net benefit or lack of sufficient data to determine a strong alignment.
*   **+1 (Complete Alignment)**: Indicates that the treatment plan is highly likely to improve the patient's condition or significantly maximize their well-being.

## Configuration

The component allows for dynamic adjustments of its internal behavior via the `valawai/c2/treatment_beneficence_valuator/control/parameters` channel. This enables the system to modulate how different beneficence criteria (e.g., survival vs. quality of life) are weighted during the valuation process.

For detailed instructions on how to deploy the component and configure its environment, please refer to the [Deploying](/docs/components/C2/treatment_beneficence_valuator/deploy) section.

## Monitoring

You can monitor the component's activity and its valuation results through the [MOV user interface](/docs/architecture/implementations/mov/wui/live#manage-logs), which allows you to track the processing of treatments and the generation of value-alignment feedback.
