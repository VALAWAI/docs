# How to use

The **Treatment Nonmaleficence Valuator** is a C2 reflective component designed to ensure that clinical treatment plans adhere to the fundamental ethical principle of "first, do no harm." By analyzing the proposed treatment alongside the patient's clinical fragility and current status, the component provides an ethical alignment score that helps clinicians identify if a plan poses an unacceptable risk of harm relative to its potential benefit.

As a reflective module, it provides a critical value-based constraint that can inhibit or modify decisions made by the C1 layer (such as the [NIT Protocol Manager](/docs/components/C1/nit_protocol_manager)) if a conflict with nonmaleficence principles is detected.

## Operational Flow

The Treatment Nonmaleficence Valuator operates within the VALAWAI reflective loop to provide real-time ethical oversight:

1.  **Treatment Reception**: The component subscribes to the channel `valawai/c2/treatment_nonmaleficence_valuator/data/treatment`. It receives treatment payloads containing the proposed actions and the detailed status of the patient.
2.  **Nonmaleficence Evaluation**: The valuator analyzes specific criteria to identify potential harm, including:
    *   **Fragility and Risk**: Analysis of the `frail_VIG` index and the `clinical_risk_group` to determine if the proposed actions are too aggressive for the patient's physical state.
    *   **Therapeutic Intensity**: Evaluating the `nit_level` of the treatment to ensure it does not exceed the threshold of "futile" or "harmful" care given the patient's `expected_survival`.
    *   **Status Deterioration**: Comparing the `before_status` with the `expected_status` to ensure the treatment does not inadvertently lead to a worse clinical state or increased discomfort.
    *   **Cronic Complexity**: Considering the `ccd` (Complex Cronic Disease) status to assess the risk of systemic complications from specific treatment actions.
3.  **Value Calculation**: It translates these risk factors into a quantifiable alignment score.
4.  **Feedback Generation**: The resulting alignment value is published to the channel `valawai/c2/treatment_nonmaleficence_valuator/data/treatment_value_feedback`.

## Understanding the Alignment Score

The feedback generated by the Treatment Nonmaleficence Valuator is visualized in the [Patient Treatment UI](/docs/components/C0/patient_treatment_ui/usage.md) as a horizontal bar chart. The score ranges from **-1 to +1**:

*   **-1 (Strong Conflict)**: Indicates a significant risk of harm (e.g., an aggressive treatment proposed for a highly frail patient where the potential for suffering outweighs any probable benefit).
*   **0 (Neutral)**: Indicates no significant risk of harm or lack of sufficient data to determine a strong alignment.
*   **+1 (Complete Alignment)**: Indicates that the treatment plan is safe and the potential for harm is minimized or acceptable relative to the goals of care.

## Configuration

The component allows for dynamic adjustments of its internal behavior via the `valawai/c2/treatment_nonmaleficence_valuator/control/parameters` channel. This enables the system to modulate how different nonmaleficence criteria (e.g., fragility vs. therapeutic intensity) are weighted during the valuation process.

For detailed instructions on how to deploy the component and configure its environment, please refer to the [Deploying](/docs/components/C2/treatment_nonmaleficence_valuator/deploy) section.

## Monitoring

You can monitor the component's activity and its valuation results through the [MOV user interface](/docs/architecture/implementations/mov/wui/live#manage-logs), which allows you to track the processing of treatments and the generation of value-alignment feedback.
