# Treatment Nonmaleficence Valuator

The **Treatment Nonmaleficence Valuator (C2)** is a reflective component designed to ensure that clinical treatment plans adhere to the fundamental ethical principle of "first, do no harm." By analyzing proposed treatments alongside the patient's clinical fragility and current status, the component provides an ethical alignment score that helps clinicians identify if a plan poses an unacceptable risk of harm relative to its potential benefit.

As a reflective module, it provides a critical value-based constraint that can inhibit or modify decisions made by the C1 layer if a conflict with nonmaleficence principles is detected.
